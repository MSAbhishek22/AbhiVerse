🛡 Secure, and audited — not just claimed –▢✕

Nobody gets in unless you say so.

On 25 Oct 2026 the codebase passed a full source audit (read the report). The short version:

  • Keys never leave your devices. Secure storage on the phone, a locked file on Windows. Never uploaded, never logged.
  • Every request is signed. Fakes and replays are refused automatically.
  • Nothing is automatic. Pairing, sessions and remote input need your explicit approval on the laptop. Anything unsigned or unpaired is blocked and logged — only you can approve or revoke it.
  • Zero telemetry. No analytics, ads or tracking. The app talks only to the address you configure.
  • Instant kill switch. Revoke a device and its live sessions end immediately.

One advisory from the audit: plain HTTP is allowed for trusted local-network testing — always use the HTTPS address for a hosted setup.

📲 Install in minutes –▢✕

From zero to remote in three steps

1
On the laptop (Windows):
Download the release, run the API, then abhiverse-agent --pair and keep the window open.
2
On the phone (Android):
Install the APK, point it at your API address, scan the laptop's QR code.
3
Approve on the laptop
when prompted. Your laptop appears in the app — ready to control.

Fastest path: scripts\run-real-device-test.ps1 builds everything and opens pairing in one command. Full guide: docs/real-device-setup.md.
